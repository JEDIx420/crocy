# CROCY — Water & Surface Interaction System Specification

## 1. Current Implementation (V0.1)
- Single horizontal `PlaneGeometry(240, 240)` at `Y = 0.0`.
- Standard PBR material (`TropicalWater.ts`) with fixed opacity (`0.82`) and uniform roughness oscillation.
- Zero localized wake, ripples, splashes, or dynamic water displacement.

---

## 2. Planned Upgrades (Phase 2)

### A. Water Surface Material & Shading
- Multi-octave scrolling normal maps simulating gentle river surface ripples and wind chop.
- Depth-based transparency: shallow water allows visibility of riverbed sand/mud; deep channels darken to brackish emerald green (`#0e2a25`).
- Soft shoreline boundary blending: depth buffer intersection fading to prevent hard clipping against mudbanks.

### B. Dynamic Ripple & Wake System
- **Pooled Ripple System:** Bounded ring buffer of active ripples generated by:
  - Swimming tail propulsion (sine pulse behind crocodile).
  - Paws entering/exiting water in wading shallows.
  - Submerging / surfacing transitions.
  - Disturbance from fleeing fish and crabs.
- **Continuous V-Shaped Swimming Wake:**
  - Emitted from crocodile snout and shoulders during surface movement (`speed > 0.5 m/s`).
  - Expanding wedge geometry that fades in opacity and disperses outward.

### C. Underwater Visuals & Transitions
- Hysteresis boundary at `Y = 0.0` (±0.05m threshold) to prevent rapid fog toggling when the camera skims the surface.
- Submerged post-effects: dense murky green fog (`0x0c2522`, density `0.065`), caustic lighting simulation, and subtle suspended sediment particle drift.
- Caustics projection on shallow mudbanks and riverbed.
